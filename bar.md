# bar.md — mechanisms extracted from the reference (Vivid+Co / vividand.co)

The craft critic judges rendered screenshots against these seven checkable mechanisms.
Every line is verifiable by looking. Adjectives are not mechanisms.

1. **No weight, only scale.** No font weight above 400 anywhere on the page; the display
   line is at least 6× the body size and that ratio alone carries hierarchy.
2. **Two neutral inks.** Exactly two neutral type colors (#EDE6DA and #8A8172). Pure
   R/G/B appears only inside the channel-split artifact, grade-reveal fringes, RGB-parade
   divider, or channel plates — never on a button, link, icon, or hover state.
3. **Flat by design.** Zero box-shadows and zero border-radius anywhere; depth reads only
   through band contrast (#0B0A08 vs #14120E) and 1px #262219 hairlines.
4. **One curve, two properties.** A single easing curve in the whole stylesheet; only
   transform and opacity animate; no transition or animation shorter than 300ms.
5. **Negative space is structural.** Hero is ≥ 40% empty viewport; gaps between sections
   ≥ 96px; sections carried by type alone outnumber sections carrying images.
6. **No raw image.** Every image sits under a darkening gradient and the grain overlay;
   nothing looks like a pasted-in AI render.
7. **Motion converges.** Every animation settles in one direction toward alignment or
   rest — nothing bounces, overshoots, loops visibly (except the ≤3px ambient channel
   drift), or moves faster than the "focus pull" curve allows.
