Cordillera Cordillera Cordillera Cordillera

ARRI ALEXA 35 · 2.39:1 · Rec.709 / Dolby Vision · dir. Valentina Reyes

A salt hauler crosses the Atacama. The light never once helps him.

The film — Cordillera was shot over five weeks at four thousand meters, almost entirely in natural light. A desert western: one man, one truck, and the whitest ground on Earth.

The problem — High-altitude sun is merciless. Chalk highlights, black shadows, and a UV haze that pushes every wide shot toward blue. The dailies looked thin — correct, but thin.

The approach — We built a single LUT from Ektachrome 100 references and held it for the whole film. From there, printer-light offsets per reel; nothing keyframed. The third act’s day-for-dusk was rescued in the grade — two stops down, highlights held, the red channel lifted just far enough to keep skin alive.
