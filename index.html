02 / Oficio

The craft

Grading is subtraction. The image already contains its best version — our work is to remove everything that isn’t it.

  • /01 Feature grading Look development through final grade. One colorist, start to credits. See it done on Cordillera.
  • /02 Commercial finishing Grade, beauty, and delivery for broadcast and social — every aspect, every codec.
  • /03 HDR & Dolby Vision Trim passes that respect the SDR master instead of imitating it.
  • /04 On-set DIT & look development Show LUTs built before the first slate, so the set sees what the film will be.
  • /05 Remote sessions Calibrated review from anywhere, live from our suite in Santiago.

03 / Estudio

One room, one colorist

One suite in Barrio Italia, Santiago. A calibrated projector, black walls, a silent room, and time. We take fewer projects than we are offered, and we finish every one of them ourselves.

SALAR is led by senior colorist Emilia Contreras — twelve years of features and commercials across Santiago, Buenos Aires, and Mexico City. She grades every frame that carries the studio’s name.
